# Hundred Steps to Life — Visual Direction

## Three possible approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| The Quiet Ascent | A warm, editorial learning journal with rich paper tones and deliberate pace. It makes progress feel thoughtful rather than gamified. | 0.04 |
| Field Notes | A practical explorer's notebook with trail markers, stamped milestones, and an earned sense of forward motion. It treats life-building as a journey into capable adulthood. | 0.08 |
| Luminous Study | A calm contemporary learning room that pairs deep ink, softened light, and tactile cards. It feels focused, hopeful, and quietly premium. | 0.02 |

## Chosen approach: Field Notes

### Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial field-guide design**, informed by physical trail journals, book marginalia, and modern educational products. The interface feels like a personal route through real life, not a corporate dashboard or a cartoon game.

### Core Principles

1. **Progress is travelled, not counted.** The course map uses a winding path with waypoints rather than an imposing productivity grid.
2. **Warm discipline.** The product is clear and rigorous about action while using calm, non-shaming language and gentle visual feedback.
3. **Useful materiality.** Paper grain, stamped markers, rule lines, and slightly imperfect forms give the experience a lived-in, personal quality.
4. **One thing at a time.** The Today screen prioritises the current lesson and action, avoiding dense dashboards and competing metrics.

### Color Philosophy

The foundation is **warm parchment and midnight ink**, creating the intimacy of a notebook and the clarity of serious study. A deep **moss green** is the signature colour: it communicates steady growth without the synthetic energy of bright neon. Clay, soft gold, and sky accents appear sparingly to distinguish rewards, phases, and states without fragmenting the visual system.

### Layout Paradigm

The mobile experience is a **vertical field journal**: the current task appears as a pinned page, while progress is a curved route visible above and below it. Desktop opens into a split composition—an information rail on the left and the active page on the right—rather than a centralised dashboard. Course-map nodes deliberately vary in position to create a journey.

### Signature Elements

1. A hand-stamped **waypoint insignia** for each day and achievement.
2. A **threaded route line** that connects course milestones and subtly fills as the user progresses.
3. **Margin notes** that surface the key principle, XP, and small reminders without interrupting the lesson.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel intentional and direct. Completing a meaningful step produces a stamp, a short confirmation, and a clear change in the route—not an exaggerated celebration. Choices have satisfying press feedback. Wrong answers are treated as a cue to revisit the idea, not a failure state.

### Animation

Motion is restrained and physical. A route line can draw forward during completion, cards can settle in with a 180–240ms transform-and-opacity transition, and earned stamps can arrive with a brief 0.96-to-1 scale. No automatic bouncing or perpetual animation. All non-essential motion must be disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Fraunces** provides thoughtful, literary emphasis for phase names and major lesson titles. **Manrope** provides a clean, highly readable interface voice for lessons, actions, and controls. Headings are compact and strong; body text is generous in line height; all controls use sentence case.

### Brand Essence

**Hundred Steps to Life is a daily field guide for people building a more grounded, useful, and meaningful life.**

Personality: **grounded, purposeful, encouraging**.

### Brand Voice

The voice is clear, calm, and accountable. It never flatters empty activity or uses harsh self-criticism. Headlines are practical and memorable; calls to action invite the next honest step.

Example: “**Today is not a test of your worth. It is one chance to practise what matters.**”

Example: “**Take the next useful step. The whole path does not need solving today.**”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a bold, abstract **stepping-stone and rising path**: three irregular, stacked forms create a subtle upward route within a circular waypoint. It carries no text, stays recognisable at favicon scale, and sits next to a custom editorial wordmark.

### Signature Brand Color

**Waypoint Moss — #356A4B**. This deep, natural green anchors every active step and serves as the distinctive colour of the project.

## Style Decisions

- Progress defaults to a winding, threaded route with stamped waypoint markers. Linear bars remain secondary and never lead the emotional experience.
- Desktop layouts prioritise an active lesson page and supporting margin notes over equal-weight analytics widgets.
- XP and streak data remain available for the course loop, but are expressed as subdued **practice marks** and **returns**. Stamps, steps, notes, route progress, and milestones carry the primary reward language.
- Materiality is a product-wide system: ruled paper lines, field labels, pinned notes, understated dashed routes, and imperfect waypoint forms should support the interface without becoming decorative clutter.
